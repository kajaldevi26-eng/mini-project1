My Favorite Dish - Chole Bhature

About My Favorite Dish

My favorite dish is Chole Bhature. It is a popular Indian food. Chole is made from chickpeas, and Bhature is a soft, fluffy fried bread. It is very tasty and delicious.

Ingredients

  • Chickpeas
  • Flour
  • Onion
  • Tomato
  • Spices
  • Oil
  • Salt

Instructions

  1. Soak the chickpeas overnight.
  2. Boil the chickpeas until they are soft.
  3. Prepare a gravy using onion, tomato, and spices.
  4. Add the chickpeas to the gravy and cook well.
  5. Prepare the bhature dough using flour.
  6. Roll the dough and fry the bhature until fluffy.
  7. Serve hot chole bhature.

Tip: Serve Chole Bhature hot for the best taste.
